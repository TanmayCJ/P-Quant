# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want P-Quant to be a welcoming, harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Good behaviour includes:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experience
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Disagreeing about results with evidence, not with people
- Owning mistakes, including in your own runs, and correcting them openly
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission
- Submitting fabricated or knowingly misleading results
- Promoting trading products, signals or investment schemes
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone is
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **tannycjain@gmail.com**. All reports will be
reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful.
The usual steps are: a private warning, then a temporary ban, then a permanent ban for repeated
or serious violations.
